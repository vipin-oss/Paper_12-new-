\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}
%% ===========================================================================

A two-dimensional $C^1$ Bogner--Fox--Schmit element has been developed for
piezoelectric--flexoelectric strips in which the strain-gradient energy is
generated by the second-moment tensor $M$ of a rectangular averaging window,
the electric enthalpy carries the piezoelectric and the flexoelectric couplings
on one footing, and the dielectric memory is a fractional Kelvin--Voigt law of
Caputo order $\alpha$ discretized by the L1 scheme on reduced modal
coordinates. The formulation was verified against exact identities and
analytical limits, and used to answer four design questions.

\begin{enumerate}
\item The window enters only through $M$: equal-second-moment windows give
  identical solutions, so the shape is not a parameter, while the anisotropy
  and the orientation are worth $15$--$19\,\%$ in the extensional coupling.
\item The extensional coupling of the \SI{200}{\micro\metre}$\times$
  \SI{10}{\micro\metre} strip is $0.5581$, within $0.5\,\%$ of the closed form
  $0.5608$ and constant to $0.09\,\%$ between \SI{10}{\micro\metre} and
  \SI{1}{\micro\metre}.
\item The flexoelectric bending coupling grows by a factor $24$ over the same
  range, with a local slope from $-1.96$ to $-0.43$, and reaches
  $4.2\times10^{-3}$, two orders of magnitude below the extensional coupling;
  the design map of Sec.~\ref{sec:designmap} shows that the window
  half-thickness against the strip thickness is the variable that sets it.
\item The two electrode configurations are not additive: at equal tip
  displacement the extensional arrangement gives $58$ times the open-circuit
  voltage, and the bending arrangement collects no piezoelectric charge at all.
\item At $\alpha=1$ the short-circuit memory damps $0.450$ times as much as the
  condensed one, and the classical Kelvin--Voigt limit is reproduced to
  $0.02\,\%$; at $\alpha<1$ the free decay is not exponential and only a
  first-period decrement is defined.
\end{enumerate}

The framework is intended for the design of thin piezoelectric--flexoelectric
sensing strips: which electrode arrangement to use, over which thickness range
the flexoelectric term matters, how the window should be oriented, and what
damping to expect at the operating point once $\alpha$, $\tau$ and the memory
matrix are stated. The two inputs assumed here rather than measured -- the
flexoelectric coefficient and the density -- fix the absolute coupling and the
frequency scale, and every ratio reported above is independent of them.
